# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary readers are developers, software architects, and the people building
the system being described. They use the rendered PDF while learning, reviewing,
and reasoning about an unfamiliar or evolving system.

## Product Purpose

The design layer turns a system's architecture into a durable, navigable model.
It should disclose complexity progressively: establish the whole-system picture,
show the recurring principles and patterns that shape it, then let the reader
descend through systems, domains, components, interactions, and implementation-
relevant detail without losing orientation.

Success means that a technical reader can understand both how the system fits
together and why its parts repeatedly take the forms they do.

## Positioning

The design layer is authored as structured Typst that calls one checked renderer
library directly. Its visual document and its mechanical contracts are two views
of the same model rather than a diagramming layer applied after the design was
written.

## Operating Context

Authors maintain `design.typ`, `CONTEXT.typ`, and architecture decision records
beside a codebase. Readers consume a single generated PDF that spans the root
design, multiple bounded contexts, a contents map, and a glossary. The document
must support both first-pass orientation and repeated reference during design and
implementation work.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The output is a deterministic A4 PDF generated by Typst.
- The authoring vocabulary is declared in the schema and exposed as renderer
  functions; visual improvements must preserve those contracts.
- The document contains diagrams, charts, tables, cards, pills, system
  statements, behavior descriptions, entity censuses, sequences, state machines,
  cross-references, chapter navigation, and glossary material.
- The same ideas may appear at several abstraction levels. Visual encodings must
  distinguish level, ownership, relation, status, and evidence without assigning
  competing meanings to the same cue.
- Mathematical notation is outside the present redesign scope.
- Accessibility must survive print, grayscale, and common forms of color-vision
  deficiency; color alone must not carry meaning.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the complete handwritten Typst renderer under `lib/`,
the declared vocabulary in `schema/design-schema.json`, and the comprehensive
render fixture in `fixtures/gallery.typ`. No external brand assets or existing
brand commitments have been supplied.

## Product Principles

- Preserve orientation while depth increases.
- Show the whole before asking the reader to inspect the parts.
- Encode recurring concepts consistently across every block family.
- Make abstraction level and ownership visible at a glance.
- Use progressive disclosure in the document's sequence and composition, not by
  hiding information that a static PDF reader cannot reveal interactively.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The document should remain legible when printed, viewed on ordinary screens, or
read without reliable color discrimination. Labels, shapes, line styles, order,
and typography must reinforce every semantic color cue.
